Project Overview

Nestled in the scenic hills of Banepa, this luxury villa offers breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayan range and the lush green valleys below. Designed by Engic Group, this 5BHK residence seamlessly blends modern architectural design with the natural beauty of its surroundings.

The villa features expansive glass facades that flood the interior with natural light while framing stunning mountain vistas. Every detail, from the open-plan living spaces to the private terraces, has been thoughtfully designed to maximize comfort, luxury, and connection with nature.

Built with premium materials and incorporating smart home technology, this hillside villa represents the pinnacle of luxury living in Nepal's hill stations.
